GSK3β-driven Phosphorylation of ABLIM1 Regulates Its Interactions with Titin in Cardiac Muscle

Co-Authored Research • Published 2025

Executive Synopsis

This study investigates how GSK3β-driven phosphorylation modulates the binding dynamics between actin-binding LIM protein 1 (ABLIM1) and the giant sarcolemmal protein titin in cardiac muscle cells. Utilizing a combination of molecular dynamics (MD) trajectory analysis in GROMACS and structural validation, we detail the biomechanical mechanisms governing sarcomeric organization.
