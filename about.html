About Me

I am a researcher, writer, and visual thinker fascinated by molecular dynamics and narrative design. My work combines wet-lab experimentation with computational modeling to explore complex biological systems, alongside creative writing that translates dense scientific concepts into accessible human stories.

Whether analyzing protein dynamics using GROMACS, crafting short fiction, or tutoring students in calculus and biochemistry, I aim to connect analytical precision with creative clarity.
